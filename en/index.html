Mission & Vision

Drone warfare is won not by the performance of a single airframe, but by supply chains and operational capability. KEONIX is designing a system that gives Korea both.

Vision

Additive Manufacturing × Full-Lifecycle Operations

We unify additive-manufacturing-based production and full-lifecycle operations into a single system, enabling high-speed mass production and wide operational adoption of drones at the same time.

Opportunity

Low-cost, rapid production is a strategic advantage

As drones prove their cost-exchange efficiency on the battlefield, nations are moving beyond simple price competition to secure sovereign drone production and operational capabilities.

01

Secure low-cost, rapid production technology

Develop and secure rapid, low-cost drone production technology based on additive manufacturing.

02

Build distributed production infrastructure

Miniaturize and advance production technology to expand into distributed, field-level production.

03

Scale through education & training

Build education and training programs for production and operations, and roll them out across target organizations.

Axis 01

Intelligent Drone Foundry

Design · Produce · Assemble · Operate

An integrated platform that centrally and securely manages drone design, production, assembly, application, and operation. AI automatically derives the optimal design for each situation, which is then produced and assembled through 3D printing.

Axis 02

Rapid, Field-Adaptive Production

Adaptive Drone Manufacturing

Drones are built on-site, on demand, using Speed 3D printers and modular design. We advance structural technology so airframe stiffness and flexibility can be achieved quickly and at low cost.

/01

Integrated drone operations system

Design and develop an integrated platform linking design, production, assembly, application, and operation.

/02

AI field-adaptive design & auto-assembly

Automatically derive mission-optimal designs from baseline designs and produce them via 3DP.

/03

Airframe material development & optimization

Develop and optimize mission-specific materials for FPV, ISR, counter-drone, and more.

/04

Rapid additive manufacturing technology

Develop rapid production technology based on Speed 3D printers and modular design.

/05

Advanced airframe stiffness & flexibility

Improve both stiffness and flexibility of airframe structures for low-cost, rapid production.

/06

Plug&Play airframe architecture

Research new drone airframe structures and design them for modular assembly.
